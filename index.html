01 — About

Pipelines, APIs, and the screen at the end of them

Most of my work lives at the seam between a data system and the person who has to act on it. At the Urban Institute I built React KPI dashboards on top of the same PySpark pipelines I wrote to feed them, which meant owning the whole chain — ingestion, validation, API, render — and being the one accountable when a number looked wrong.
